description=Add your own proposed roads, cycleways and paths to the OpenStreetMap network and get before/after networks back.
about=NetworkForge integrates a layer of proposed roads, cycleways and paths into the OpenStreetMap network and returns a "before" and an "after" network as QGIS layers, plus OSM PBF files for routers such as Valhalla.
    This plugin is the QGIS front end. The network logic lives in the separate NetworkForge engine (https://github.com/Yibbzz/networkforge), which the plugin installs into its own environment and runs as a separate process.
    Proof of concept. Limitations: no public transport, no traffic simulation, no turn restrictions yet.
    Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL).
